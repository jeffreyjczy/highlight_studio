Highlight Studio

Highlight Studio is a personal desktop app for Windows that turns my own gameplay recordings into short highlight clips, merges them into videos with music, and uploads the finished videos to my own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Lists gameplay recordings stored on my PC and makes highlight clips from them.
  • Merges selected clips into one video with licensed music and its credit line.
  • Uploads a merged video to my YouTube channel, only when I press Upload and after I have checked the title, description, tags and visibility.

Google and YouTube access

The app asks for permission to upload videos to YouTube and to read the channel name, so it can show which channel it's connected to. It doesn't read, change or delete any other YouTube or Google data.
